# ADR-0005: Onboarding — Free Trial First Week

**Date:** 2026-07-15
**Status:** Accepted
**Decision:** Investors receive one free week of deals before payment is required.

## Context

Investors are skeptical of paid deal services. PropStream charges $199/year for data they could get free from counties. Asking investors to commit $250+/month without seeing quality is a high barrier.

### Alternatives considered

1. **Payment upfront** — charge before first digest. Problem: high refund rate, skeptical market.
2. **Free week trial** — first digest free, then charge. Low risk for investor, proves value before commitment.
3. **Free sample deal** — send one deal before signup. Problem: doesn't prove consistency.

## Decision

First week free. Investor fills out funnel form, receives first Monday digest without charge. Card is collected at signup, charged after first digest. Investor can cancel after trial.

## Consequences

- Requires payment collection at signup (card on file)
- ~7-day delay before first revenue
- Investors who see good deals convert naturally
- Must ensure first week deals are high-quality (no "cold start" with bad matches)
